## Acknowledgments

Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel-1 data processed by Geoscience Australia. Sentinel-1 data were acquired through the European Union’s Copernicus Programme and supplied by the European Space Agency.
 
Products from this workflow have been assessed against the CEOS ARD Normalised Radar Backscatter Product Family Specification (v1.3.1) and approved by the CEOS ARD working group.

Development of this product has drawn on methods developed by NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory for the Observational Products for End-Users from Remote Sensing Analysis (OPERA) Radiometric Terrain Corrected Sentinel-1 (RTC-S1) product.

## Background

Digital Earth Antarctica produced the Antarctic SAR backscatter because users explicitly wanted it. The Digital Earth Antarctica's 2023 scoping study involved extensive stakeholder engagement with Australian government and Antarctic science users. Existing analysis-ready coverage was inadequate, particularly for EW/sea-ice observations, and Antarctica needs specialised processing to turn Sentinel-1 observations into consistent continental-scale data suitable for science, monitoring and operations.  

There is also not currently a continental scale analysis ready backscatter SAR product over Australia, which means there is opportunity to be the first to produce such a product. At the moment, researchers are processing small areas of interest themselves, but this can lead to inconsistencies or issues when the datasets are combined. Having consistent continental scale data across Australia is also needed, and desired by scientists, researchers and other government agencies

## What this product offers

The product was created to provide consistent, analysis-ready Sentinel-1 radar observations of Antarctica and Australia, enabling large-scale monitoring of ice, sea ice and environmental change, including during periods when optical satellites are limited by darkness or cloud. 

Measurement of radar backscatter from the Australian continent and the Antarctic surface, indicating moisture content, water, land cover type, surface roughness, structure and changes in ice and sea ice over time.

## Applications

The data supports surface-water monitoring, soil moisture, vegetation and land-cover analysis, coastal monitoring and infrastructure observation in Australia, and ice-sheet and ice-shelf monitoring, ice velocity and calving studies, iceberg tracking, crevasse detection, snow and sea-ice monitoring, and Antarctic operational support. SAR provides particular value where observations are affected by cloud cover or polar darkness.

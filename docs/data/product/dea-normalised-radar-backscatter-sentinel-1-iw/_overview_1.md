

## About

Geoscience Australia Sentinel-1 Interferometric Wide (IW) Normalised Radar Backscatter (NRB) is part of Digital Earth’s (DE) suite of Analysis Ready Data products. It represents the archive of Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar observations acquired in IW swath mode over the Australian continent and the Antarctic coastal zone, which have been calibrated, noise corrected and adjusted using region-specific digital elevation models (DEM) - ready for easy analysis.

## About

Hotspots shows where high levels of infrared radiation have been detected across Australia. These detections may indicate fires or other sources of heat such as industrial activity, gas flares, hot surfaces and naturally occurring phenomena. 

This information supports situational awareness and ongoing monitoring for emergency management, environmental monitoring and research.
